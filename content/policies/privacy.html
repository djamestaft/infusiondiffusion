Information we collect

We use the information needed to take and fulfil orders, process payments and refunds, deliver products, provide accounts and support, and communicate about purchases. This processing supports entering into and performing our contract with you. We also process information to meet legal obligations, keep appropriate business records, prevent fraud and protect the security of our services, relying on the applicable legal obligations or legitimate interests recognised by POPIA.

Where consent is required, including for optional tracking or electronic marketing, we seek it separately. Using the website or agreeing to our terms does not by itself give consent to all processing. We do not use your information for a materially different incompatible purpose without an appropriate lawful basis and notice.

Information needed for payment, delivery or a requested account is necessary to provide that service; if you choose not to provide it, we may be unable to complete the request. Optional information and consent choices do not affect your statutory rights.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. Necessary cookies support the cart, secure sign-in, checkout and fraud prevention. Shopify-hosted services also use analytics and marketing cookies. Optional tracking requires appropriate consent where applicable; a privacy notice alone is not a consent choice.

  • Cart: infusion_cart remembers the shopping cart for up to 14 days.
  • Sign-in: __Host-infusion-customer-login secures a sign-in attempt for up to 10 minutes; __Host-infusion-customer maintains an account session for up to seven days.
  • Shopify/Shop operation: _shopify_essential and _shop_app_essential support their services; observed lifetimes can be up to one year.
  • Shopify analytics and marketing: _shopify_s typically lasts 30 minutes; _shopify_y, _shopify_analytics and _shopify_marketing may last around one year.

Cookie names and lifetimes can change and may be renewed by activity. See Shopify's cookie information for current details. Where cookie choices are presented by Shopify at checkout or account services, use those controls to accept or reject optional categories and revisit the available privacy settings to change your choice. Our storefront and Shopify checkout may use different domains, so choices should not be assumed to carry between them.

You can also delete or block cookies through your browser. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent the cart, account or checkout from working. Deleting cookies does not itself delete order or account records. Contact Dione Smith if you cannot locate a privacy control or wish to withdraw consent.

Retention and security

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, legal recordkeeping duties, resolving disputes and enforcing agreements. Order, payment, accounting and support records can be retained longer than a website session. When information is no longer lawfully needed, it should be deleted, destroyed or de-identified in accordance with applicable requirements.

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections, restricted access and encrypted account-session data. No system can guarantee absolute security. Where a security compromise requires notification under POPIA, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected people as required.

Your rights and requests

Subject to POPIA's requirements and lawful exceptions, you may request confirmation of whether we hold your personal information, access to it, correction of inaccurate information, and deletion or destruction of information we are no longer entitled to retain. You may object to certain processing, withdraw consent for future consent-based processing, and object to direct marketing. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing already lawfully carried out.

Send requests to Dione Smith using the contact details above. We may reasonably verify your identity before disclosing or changing information. We will explain any legal reason why a request cannot be fully met and any applicable procedure or prescribed fee before proceeding.
