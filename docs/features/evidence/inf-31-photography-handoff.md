# INF-31 photography handoff

Prepared 7 September 2026 from the [approved INF-30 brief](inf-30-launch-asset-audit.md)
and [reviewed source references](inf-31-asset-shortlist.md). Coordinator: Shawnee.
Devon accepts product identity, supplier costs and final images. This is a
capture and intake checklist; no supplier has been contacted or booked.

## Capture list

The [shot intake CSV](inf-31-shot-intake.csv) contains one row per requested
image, mapped to the existing Shopify product GID. The six approved fragrance
names are carried forward; physical labels must be checked before capture.
These identifiers are product IDs, not merchant SKU values.

For each of the six products, capture:

1. A primary view of the complete diffuser with actual reeds, readable front
   label, full bottle base and tassel. Confirm the reed count with Devon.
2. A straight label/name detail.
3. A cap, tassel and seal/material detail.
4. A packaging view, conditional on Devon confirming the final supplied box.
5. A scale/context view, conditional on confirmed packaged dimensions.

This gives 18 required product images and 12 conditional images. Capture at
least three additional range-level lifestyle images in approved room contexts.
The three lifestyle rows deliberately leave product and location blank until
the actual scene is selected; additional accepted scenes can be added.

## Consistency and delivery

- Use the same camera height, focal length, bottle scale, horizon and neutral
  surface for all six primary views.
- Compose at native 4:5 or a larger portrait area. Allow square crops while
  retaining reed tips, base, tassel and label.
- Deliver masters at least 2400 pixels on the short edge. Retain the lossless
  or maximum-quality originals and provide derivatives separately.
- Control glare without changing black-glass, gold-metal or label appearance.
  Do not alter product proportions, invent ingredients, add decorative smoke
  or bake marketing text into the image.
- Lifestyle scenes need crop room and a named product. Record property and
  visible-person permissions where applicable.

Bois and Blanc isolated photographs are useful bottle references; Blanc's
event packaging image shows the diffuser with reeds. Santuaire close-ups can
guide material detail. None replaces the consistent primary set. Ambre,
Été and Noir still lack isolated real-product primary references in the
reviewed inventory. Santuaire's deferred scent notes do not prevent capture.

## Intake procedure

1. Keep the source filename. Enter its path, SHA-256, creator, capture date,
   original-master location and transformation history against the shot ID.
   An empty cell means unknown, not accepted or not applicable.
2. Record owner/licensor, permitted channels, territory, duration, restrictions
   and any release evidence for each delivered image. Earlier rights approval
   for the existing source inventory does not cover a new supplier's work.
3. Record dimensions, focal/crop guidance and factual alt text after inspecting
   the delivered image. Do not write final alt text from a planned shot alone.
4. Devon records acceptance and date. Rejected images retain their records and
   a reason; replacement files receive a new source path and checksum.
5. Accepted product media belongs in Shopify. Editorial lifestyle destinations
   are selected in Sanity when the approved composition is known. Upload and
   publication require their separate source approvals and previews.

The CSV is a working intake sheet, not a Shopify import or upload manifest.
Every row starts as pending; no empty provenance or acceptance cell can be
treated as evidence that an image is ready to publish.

## Supplier quote scope

Request a separate price for the 18 required product images, the 12 conditional
images, and at least three lifestyle images. Ask for capture and retouch costs,
studio/location and styling costs, turnaround, revision allowance, master and
derivative delivery, usage terms and any exclusions. These are quote inputs,
not a budget or booking. Confirm product/reed/packaging readiness before dates
are agreed. The approved audit's time estimates are planning estimates only.

## Remaining inputs

- Devon: physical products and labels, actual reeds, final packaging and
  packaged dimensions; source/master acceptance and any expenditure decision.
- Shawnee: coordinate available masters or capture arrangements, collect the
  per-file records and prepare source previews.
- Photographer: capture and source records under the accepted quote and rights.

Acceptance of this preparation requires all six product IDs to match the
existing manifest, complete required and conditional shot coverage, unique
shot IDs, and no invented provenance or acceptance. It does not complete
INF-31 or authorize INF-32 final design acceptance.
